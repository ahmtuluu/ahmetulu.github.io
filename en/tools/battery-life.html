Battery Life Calculator (with Sleep Mode)

Enter how long your device is awake and how long it sleeps; see the average current and how many days the battery lasts. For ESP32-style sleep projects.

How is it calculated?

A device with a sleep mode spends most of its time at a very low current. What sets battery life is the average current over one cycle.

Iavg = (Iactive · tactive + Isleep · tsleep) / (tactive + tsleep)Life (hours) = Capacity (Ah) · k / Iavgk: usable capacity ratio (margin for cut-off voltage, temperature and self-discharge)

Example: 80 mA for 5 s, then 20 µA sleep for 10 s → average ≈ 26.7 mA. Stretch the sleep to 10 minutes and the average drops to ≈ 0.68 mA, so a 2000 mAh battery lasts for months.

In practice

  • On development boards the regulator, USB-serial chip and LEDs can draw mA even in sleep; measure to be sure.
  • Usable capacity drops at high current and low temperature.
