Basics

Voltage Divider Calculator

Drop a voltage with two resistors: calculate the output, or find the resistor needed for a target voltage. Add a load resistance to see the real output.

How is it calculated?

When two resistors are in series and the output is taken from the middle, the input voltage is divided in the ratio of the resistors.

Vout = Vin · R2 / (R1 + R2)With a load: R2' = R2 · RL / (R2 + RL)R2 = R1 · Vout / (Vin − Vout)Divider current: I = Vin / (R1 + R2)

In “find bottom resistor” mode R2 is calculated, then the nearest standard values in the E24 (±5%) and E96 (±1%) series are suggested together with the real Vout you would get.

In practice

  • A divider is not a power supply: its output drops under load. The load field shows how much.
  • For measuring a battery with an ADC, a total of 10–100 kΩ is a good start; MΩ values can add noise and error.
