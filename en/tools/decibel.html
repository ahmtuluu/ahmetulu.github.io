Decibel (dB) and dBm Calculator

Express gain and attenuation in decibels: dB from a voltage or power ratio, a ratio from dB, and watts from dBm.

How is it calculated?

A decibel expresses the ratio of two quantities on a log scale. Because power is proportional to voltage squared (at the same impedance), the factor is 20 for voltage and 10 for power.

Voltage: dB = 20 · log₁₀(V2 / V1)Power: dB = 10 · log₁₀(P2 / P1)Inverse: V ratio = 10^(dB/20), P ratio = 10^(dB/10)dBm: P(W) = 10^(dBm/10) / 1000, V(50 Ω) = √(P · 50)

Worth remembering: +6 dB ≈ voltage ×2, +3 dB ≈ power ×2, +20 dB = voltage ×10. 0 dBm = 1 mW and 20 dBm = 100 mW (common for Wi-Fi modules).
