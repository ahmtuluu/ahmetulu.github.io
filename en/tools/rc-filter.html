Timing & filters

RC Filter and Time Constant Calculator

Enter R and C to see the RC filter cut-off frequency, the time constant (τ) and how long the capacitor takes to charge to the level you want.

How is it calculated?

A resistor and a capacitor form both a simple filter and a delay circuit. The same product sets both: τ = R·C.

τ = R · Cfc = 1 / (2π · R · C)Vc(t) = V · (1 − e^(−t/τ)) → t = −τ · ln(1 − p)p: target charge ratio (0.9 = 90%)

After 1τ the capacitor is at 63.2%, after 5τ at 99.3%; in practice 5τ counts as fully charged. The cut-off frequency is where signal power halves (−3 dB) and is the same for RC low-pass and high-pass filters.

Note: a load on the output changes the filter. Keep the load at least 10× larger than R.
