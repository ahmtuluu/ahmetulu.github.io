SMD Resistor and Capacitor Code Decoder

Type the code printed on an SMD resistor or ceramic capacitor and see the value instantly. Understands 3-digit, 4-digit, R-notation and EIA-96 codes.

How is it calculated?

SMD resistors

3 digits: AB C → AB × 10^C 103 = 10 × 10³ = 10 kΩ4 digits: ABC D → ABC × 10^D 1002 = 100 × 10² = 10 kΩR as decimal point: 4R7 = 4.7 Ω, R10 = 0.1 ΩEIA-96: 01C = 100 × 100 = 10 kΩ

In an EIA-96 code the two digits are the index of the value in the E96 series (01 = 100, 02 = 102 … 96 = 976). The letter is the multiplier: Z = 0.001 · Y/R = 0.01 · X/S = 0.1 · A = 1 · B/H = 10 · C = 100 · D = 1 k · E = 10 k · F = 100 k.

Ceramic capacitors

AB C → AB × 10^C pF 104 = 10 × 10⁴ pF = 100 nFC = 8 → ×0.01, C = 9 → ×0.1A trailing letter is the tolerance: B ±0.1 pF · C ±0.25 pF · D ±0.5 pF · F ±1% · G ±2% · J ±5% · K ±10% · M ±20% · Z +80/−20%
