Basics

Ohm's Law Calculator

Enter any two of V, I, R and P and the other two are calculated instantly. Pick units (mA, kΩ, mW…) from the box next to each field.

How is it calculated?

Ohm's law links the voltage (V), current (I) and resistance (R) of a resistor. Combined with the power formula, knowing any two of the four quantities is enough to find the others.

V = I · RP = V · I = I² · R = V² / R

The tool solves these equations for the pair you pick. For example, with P and R known it first finds V = √(P·R), then I = √(P/R).

In practice

  • Pick a resistor rated for at least twice the calculated power (0.2 W dissipated → 1/2 W resistor).
  • Ohm's law applies to linear (ohmic) parts. It does not apply directly to LEDs or diodes; use the LED resistor tool for those.
