About

Who I am and what I work on.

Hi, I'm Ahmet Ulu, a software and hardware developer from Kayseri, Türkiye. I work on embedded systems, IoT projects and automation. Applying machine learning to real-world problems is both my goal and my passion.

I write software in Python, C and C++, and bring hardware projects to life with boards like the Raspberry Pi, STM32 and ESP32. Seeing code run not just on a screen but inside a real system is a different feeling.

This site is my digital home, where I keep my projects, experiences and what I've learned in one place.
