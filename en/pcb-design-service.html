What is a PCB?

PCB stands for printed circuit board. It is the green board covered with small parts that you see inside your phone, boiler or washing machine.

When you first try a circuit, the parts usually go on a breadboard and are connected with jumper wires. That is great for experimenting, but wires come loose and the circuit is big and messy. On a PCB the connections are copper tracks printed on the board: no wires, the parts are soldered on, and the board fits neatly in an enclosure.

When do you need a PCB?

Your prototype works, but it is a tangle of wires

Let us move the circuit from the breadboard to a single board that fits in a box and does not fall apart when it shakes.

You need more than one

If you are building 5, 50 or 500 of them, factory-made identical boards beat wiring each one by hand.

School or capstone project

A tidy, professional-looking board makes a big difference in front of a jury and lowers the risk of failure.

You have a product idea

Let us design a small, low-cost board that is ready for production for the device you want to sell.

Example board types

  • Arduino / ESP32 / STM32 based control boards
  • Relay, MOSFET and motor driver outputs
  • Sensor and button input boards
  • Power input and regulator circuits
  • LED, display and traffic light boards
  • Boards that combine existing modules into one

How does it work?

FAQ

Is a PCB the same as a circuit board?

Yes. PCB stands for "printed circuit board"; people also just call it a circuit board.

I have no technical background. Can I still order one?

Yes. Describe what it should do in your own words; we will work out the technical details together.

Do you manufacture the board?

I prepare the design and the production files. The board itself is made by a PCB manufacturer, and I will explain how to order it.

How long does it take and how much does it cost?

It depends on the size and complexity of the board. Tell me about your project and I will give you a time frame and a price.

Which software do you use?

KiCad. It is free and open source, so you or anyone else can open and edit your files later.
