Why STM32, why a rocket?

Flight computer

The rocket's brain: it reads sensors, computes altitude and triggers recovery at apogee. STM32 is one of the most common MCUs on these boards.

Timing is critical

A flight is over in seconds. Interrupts, timers and DMA are needed so that no data is missed.

Register level

Knowing what is under the libraries makes it much easier to find the fault when something goes wrong in the field.
