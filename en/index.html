What is a PCB?

From a tangle of wires to one board

A PCB (printed circuit board) is the green board inside electronic devices. Instead of wires, thin copper tracks on the board connect the parts. Your circuit gets smaller and sturdier, and you can make as many copies as you need.

  • Turning an Arduino / ESP32 prototype into a product
  • School and capstone projects
  • Relay, sensor and motor boards
  • 10 or 100 copies of the same board
